- Anker states that its laptop power bank reached Top 1 sales in related power bank categories on Shopee and Lazada in Malaysia. [D15]

## Rules that shape who to recruit

- Phone chargers and other portable battery chargers up to 24 V, and chargers for IT equipment up to 20 V, need Energy Commission approval before they are sold or advertised. [D02]
- The approval is held by a Malaysian importer: every imported batch is tested by SIRIM and each unit carries an ST-SIRIM label. [D03]
- Wireless chargers are communications equipment and also need MCMC certification. [D04]
- Power banks are not named in the published list; its nearest rows are portable battery chargers up to 24 V and portable power stations, so confirm with the Energy Commission. [D05]

So the first call in this sub-category is the Malaysian company that holds the approvals: a brand's local distributor or the brand's own Malaysian company. One conversation can bring several approved models at once.
