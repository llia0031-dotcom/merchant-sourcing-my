# Outreach templates (English)

Templates only. None of these messages has been sent to anyone, and no brand in the candidate pool has been
contacted. Words in `{braces}` are filled in per brand from its row in the pool; every fact put into a message
should be one that row already backs with a source.

Which template for which row of the pool (`pool/scores.csv`, column `segment`):

| Segment | Start with |
|---|---|
| open a shop | 1 (or 1a), then 2 |
| activate | 1b, then 2 |
| grow | 1b, adapted to the shop's own numbers |
| check first | none yet: find the Malaysian contact first |

Tone rules used for all templates:
- Lead with what the brand gains and what help is offered, in plain words; no pressure, no deadlines that do not exist.
- Mention only public facts about the brand (from its own site or public news), never guesses about its sales.
- One clear next step per message.
- Do not promise traffic, fees, subsidies or approval outcomes; point to the published programme terms instead.

the first weeks: category documents, listing set-up and a first content plan with local creators.

Would a 20-minute call in the next two weeks be useful? I can share the document checklist for {subcategory}
beforehand so nothing is a surprise.

Best regards,
{your_name}
{team}, {platform}
